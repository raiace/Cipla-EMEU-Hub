Section 03 · Dining

Dining, Pubs & Eating Out

The pub is not just a bar. It is an institution — and refusing to go is a professional mistake.

🍽️ Where to Eat

  • 🥪 Casual lunch (Pret, Leon, Itsu) → £6–12
  • 🍺 Pub lunch → £12–18, great atmosphere
  • 🍜 Mid-range dinner (Wagamama, Dishoom) → £20–35
  • 🍽️ Business dinner → £40–70 per person
  • 📌 Dishoom = London's most popular Indian — always book ahead
  • 🍛 South Indian → Saravana Bhavan, London
  • 🕌 Halal → widely available in London, Birmingham, Manchester

💳 Tipping & Bills

  • 12.5% service charge often added — check bill first
  • If no service charge → tip 10–15% for good service
  • At pub bar → do NOT tip bartender — not expected
  • Split bills → common and perfectly accepted
  • Cash → almost never needed — card everywhere

🍺 Pub Culture

  • Going after work is normal and expected
  • Non-drinkers → soft drink is fine, no comment made
  • Rounds → one person buys for the whole group, turns rotate
  • Pub quiz → great team bonding activity

Section 04 · Housing

Housing & Accommodation

Where to stay, what to expect, and how to handle it when things go wrong at 11 pm on a Tuesday.

🏨 Best option: Hotel / Serviced Apt

  • Premier Inn or Marriott — reliable, well-located, good Wi-Fi
  • Citadines and Saco (serviced apartments) for 2–4 weeks
  • Good areas: City of London, Canary Wharf, South Bank
  • Costs usually confirmed and arranged by Cipla HR

💡 Short Stay Tips

  • Download Citymapper before you arrive
  • Sainsbury's Local or Tesco Express for quick groceries
  • Keep HR contact saved — they manage the booking logistics
  • Card payments accepted almost everywhere — cash rarely needed
⚠️ July–August: Book early or expect a very tough search. This is London's peak rental season — over 100,000 students flood the market before the new academic year, corporate relocations spike, and families move during school holidays, all competing for the same limited housing stock. Demand can push rents 5–10% higher than winter rates, and desirable properties go fast. If your assignment falls in this window, confirm accommodation with Cipla HR at least 6–8 weeks before arrival.

🏢 Serviced Apartment (recommended)

  • Fully furnished, bills included, flexible term
  • Cost: £1,800–£3,500/month for 1BR
  • Best areas: Canary Wharf, South Bank, Farringdon, Shoreditch, Victoria
  • Chains: Saco, Citadines, Native, Locke Hotels

🏠 Private Rental (3+ months)

  • Requires: income proof, references, deposit (5–6 weeks rent)
  • HR should provide employment letter and support
  • Platforms: Rightmove, Zoopla, SpareRoom
  • London market is tight — start search 4–6 weeks before arrival

🏥 NHS Healthcare

  • Free healthcare for all, including visa holders who paid the Immigration Health Surcharge
  • Register with a local GP using your UK address — takes a few days
  • Urgent Care Centres → no appointment needed for non-emergencies
  • Emergency → A&E or call 999
  • Private healthcare → Cipla provides insurance — use for faster access

📋 Before You Arrive

  • Confirm accommodation booking with Cipla HR
  • Check Cipla health insurance scope
  • Bring a 3-month supply of regular medications
  • Download: Citymapper, Uber, NHS App
  • Save Cipla HR contact + 999 immediately on Day 1

🗺️ Understanding London Travel Zones

London's Tube, Overground and rail are divided into 9 concentric fare zones — Zone 1 is central London, zones increase as you move outward. Your fare depends on how many zones you cross. Buses are not zoned — always £1.75 flat.

Master this table and you will navigate every meeting, every email, every feedback session. The British do not say what they mean.

🌦 Weather as Social Glue

Commenting on the weather is not a complaint — it is an invitation to connect. Always have a weather opinion ready. "Typical British weather" is a safe universal response.

🤝 Personal Space & Touch

British people maintain more physical distance than Indians. Handshakes on first meeting — no hugs. Do not stand too close. This is not unfriendliness — it is cultural norm.

Section 09 · Work Culture

The British Office — What Nobody Tells You

Walk in and everything looks familiar. Desks. Computers. Coffee machines. But beneath the surface, the rules are completely different.

01

⏰ Punctuality is respect

Meetings start on time. Arriving 5 minutes late is noticed. Joining a video call 2 minutes early is considered professional. Nine o'clock means nine o'clock.

02

💬 Understatement is communication

When a British colleague says "that's quite interesting" they may mean "I have serious concerns." Learn to read between the lines — directness is often seen as aggressive.

03

🤝 Hierarchy is flat

Senior people are addressed by first name. Take initiative and speak up in meetings. Silence is read as having nothing to contribute — participate actively.

For Indian Professionals — Read This Carefully

Less formal than Indian corporates — first name basis from Day 1
Feedback is indirect — "perhaps we could consider" means "this needs to change"
Meetings have agendas — read them before and come prepared with points
Do not interrupt — wait for a natural pause before speaking
After-work drinks are networking — attendance matters more than drinking
Small talk is a skill — weather, weekend plans, football are safe topics
Reliability is currency — do what you say you will do, when you said you'd do it

"In the UK, what is left unsaid is often more important than what is said. The ability to read the room is a professional competency, not a soft skill."

🔐 UK GDPR & Data Protection — Applies From Day 1

UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018 (post-Brexit framework)

Following Brexit, the UK operates under its own UK GDPR (which closely mirrors EU GDPR) plus the Data Protection Act 2018, enforced by the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office). If you follow EU GDPR principles, you are compliant in the UK. These rules apply to you from Day 1 of your assignment.

  • → Recording any meeting or call requires explicit consent from all participants — always ask first
  • → Colleague personal data (salary, health, HR matters) must be kept strictly confidential
  • → Do not store work-related personal data of UK employees or clients on personal devices
  • → Use only Cipla-approved tools for work communications — follow IT security guidelines
  • → Any suspected data breach must be reported to Cipla IT within 72 hours (legal requirement under UK GDPR)
